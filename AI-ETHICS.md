## AI Ethics & Responsible Use for Product Managers:
Ethical AI use means being transparent, protecting privacy, validating outputs, maintaining accountability, and always putting users first.

----

### Transparency & Attribution
🔍 Disclose AI Assistance: Be transparent when AI significantly contributed to deliverables (PRDs, research analysis)
📝 Attribute Sources: Always cite original research, customer quotes, and data sources - don't present AI summaries as primary research
🤝 Team Collaboration: Make it clear when documents are AI-generated drafts requiring team review vs. final decisions

---

### Ethical Decision Framework
**When unsure about AI usage, ask yourself:**
- Transparency Test: Would I be comfortable disclosing this AI usage publicly?
- Harm Test: Could this usage harm users, colleagues, or the company?
- Authenticity Test: Am I maintaining genuine relationships and honest communication?
- Responsibility Test: Am I taking full accountability for the outputs and decisions?
- Privacy Test: Am I protecting sensitive data and respecting consent?
- Fairness Test: Could this perpetuate bias or exclude important perspectives?

---

### Data Privacy & Security
🔒 No Sensitive Data: Never input customer PII, proprietary code, confidential financials, or competitive secrets
👥 Customer Confidentiality: Anonymize customer names, company details, and identifying information in feedback analysis
📊 Data Minimization: Share only what's necessary for the task - avoid uploading entire databases
🛡️ Compliance Awareness: Understand your company's AI usage policies and data governance requirements
💼 Enterprise Considerations: Use enterprise AI tools with appropriate data protection for sensitive work

---

### Bias & Fairness
⚖️ Question Assumptions: AI may reflect biases in training data - critically evaluate recommendations about user segments
🌍 Inclusive Design: Don't rely solely on AI for accessibility, internationalization, or underrepresented user needs
🔍 Diverse Validation: Test AI-generated personas and user stories against real diverse user research
📊 Representative Data: Ensure feedback analysis doesn't over-weight vocal minorities or ignore underserved segments
🚫 Avoid Stereotyping: Review AI-generated user personas for stereotypes or reductive characterizations

---

### Accountability & Decision-Making
👤 PM Responsibility: You own all decisions - AI is a tool, not a decision-maker or scapegoat
🎯 Critical Decisions: Never outsource high-stakes decisions (pricing, hiring, major pivots) to AI
📋 Audit Trail: Document how AI was used in important decisions for future reference
🤔 Question Recommendations: Challenge AI suggestions rather than accepting them at face value
💡 Maintain Judgment: Don't let AI convenience replace critical thinking and product intuition

---

### Customer & User Respect
💬 Authentic Engagement: AI should enhance, not replace, direct customer conversations
🎤 Real Voices: Prioritize actual customer quotes over AI-generated summaries in stakeholder communications
🔐 Consent Awareness: Ensure customer feedback usage aligns with privacy policies and consent
🎯 Value Creation: Use AI to better serve users, not to manipulate or exploit them
📱 Human Touch: Maintain personal connections in user research, support, and relationship building

---

### Red Lines - Never Use AI For
❌ Fabricating customer feedback, quotes, or research data
❌ Bypassing required legal, security, or compliance reviews
❌ Manipulating users through dark patterns or deceptive practices
❌ Replacing genuine customer empathy with synthetic analysis
❌ Intellectual property violations (using AI to recreate competitor IP)
❌ Creating discriminatory features or excluding user groups
