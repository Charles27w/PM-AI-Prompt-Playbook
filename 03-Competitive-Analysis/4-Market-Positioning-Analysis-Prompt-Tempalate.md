# 📊 Prompt Template - Market Positioning Analysis

## Purpose
Generate Market Position analysis for products and features, identifying strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and strategic positioning.

---

## 🎯 Core Competitive Analysis Prompt

### Prompt Template

Analyze how [COMPETITOR] positions themselves:
- Market Segment: [TARGET MARKET]
- Product/Feature: [SPECIFIC PRODUCT OR FEATURE OR INDUSTRY]
- Target customer segments
- Value proposition
- Messaging and brand
- Market perception
- Gaps in positioning we can exploit
- Our differentiation opportunity (1 paragraph)
- Threat level: Low/Medium/High with 1 sentence why
