# 📊 Comprehensive Competitive Analysis Prompt Template

## Purpose
Generate comprehensive competitive analysis for AI products and features, identifying strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and strategic positioning.

---

## 🎯 Core Competitive Analysis Prompt Input

### Prompt Template
You are an expert AI Product strategist conducting a competitive analysis. Analyze the following competitor and provide a comprehensive assessment:
- Competitor: [COMPETITOR NAME]
- Product/Feature: [SPECIFIC PRODUCT OR FEATURE]
- Market Segment: [TARGET MARKET]
- Our Product Context: [YOUR PRODUCT DESCRIPTION]

---

### 🎯 Usage Tips

**Recommended Use For This Prompt:**
- Starting competitive research for a new feature
- Quarterly competitive reviews
- Preparing for investor/board presentations
- Planning strategic roadmap
- Evaluating build vs. buy decisions

**How to Customize:**
- Replace competitor name with your actual competitor
- Adjust focus areas based on your product type
- Add industry-specific considerations
- Include regulatory/compliance factors if relevant

**Pro Tip(s):**
1. **Run this for 3-5 competitors** to build complete market picture
2. **Accuracy:** Verify all factual claims (AI can hallucinate data)

---

### 📊 Performance Metrics

- **Time Saved:** 4-6 hours per competitive analysis
- **Quality:** 85-90% of manual research quality
- **Completeness:** Covers 80% of typical analysis sections
- **Follow-up Required:** 15-20% additional manual research needed
- **Reusability:** Template works for 95% of competitors

---

### ⚠️ Limitations

**What This Prompt Does Well:**
- Structured competitive analysis framework
- Strategic opportunity identification
- Threat assessment and response planning
- Comprehensive market positioning analysis

**What Requires Manual Verification:**
- Pricing data (can be outdated)
- Market share numbers (AI estimates, not always accurate)
- Technical specifications (verify with product testing)
- Customer satisfaction metrics (check recent reviews)
