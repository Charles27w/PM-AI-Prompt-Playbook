# Contributing to AI PM Prompt Library

## How to Contribute

1. Test your prompt with at least 2 AI models
2. Document actual results
3. Include usage tips and performance notes
4. Follow existing template structure

## Prompt Quality Standards

- ✅ Clear and specific instructions
- ✅ Includes example input/output
- ✅ Tested and verified
- ✅ Documented limitations

Thank you for helping improve AI PM workflows! 🚀
