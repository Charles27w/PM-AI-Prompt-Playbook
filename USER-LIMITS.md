## ℹ️ Usage & Limitations

**Remember:** AI is a productivity multiplier for product managers, not a replacement for product judgment, customer empathy, or strategic thinking.

----

## ✍️ Usage Tips
- Start with the base template and adjust for your context
- Test multiple variations to find optimal phrasing
- Document what works best for your use cases
- Combine prompts for complex workflows

** ⌨️ General Prompting Use:**
- Start a new chat for each feature/product
- Verify all factual claims (AI can hallucinate data)
- Analysis - Competitive/Comparison/Research/Market
- Evaluating build vs. buy decisions
- Combine template and customized values in your prompt
- Add industry-specific considerations
- Include regulatory/compliance factors if relevant
- Documentation Speed: Use AI to accelerate first-draft creation, then refine with input

** 🚀 Pro Tips:**
1. Follow company or client AI Policies
2. Utitlize built-in GEN AI chats avaiable within your tech stack (ex: Jira's Rovo)
3. Include "Avoid use of em dases (-)" in your prompt
4. Provide formating insructions (use of italic/bold font, bullets, numbering, etc.)
5. Consider your audience / Create customized materials for different audience needs
6. More details/inputs can be helpful in obtaining desired results
7. Communicate as if the AI Chat is a person
8. Knowledge Synthesis: Combine research from multiple sources into coherent insights

### ⚠️ Limitations & Important Notes

**What Prompts Do Well:**
- Document Creation: Technical documentation, PRDs, Research
- PRDs & Specifications: Comprehensive product requirement documents, technical specifications, feature briefs
- Research & Synthesis: Combining information from multiple sources into coherent summaries
- User Research Analysis: Processing interview transcripts, survey data, feedback categorization, theme identification
- Roadmap Documentation: Release plans, feature prioritization frameworks, go-to-market strategies
- Scenario Planning: Generate multiple "what-if" scenarios for product decisions
- Stakeholder Communication: Executive summaries, status updates, launch announcements, change logs
- Competitive Analysis: Feature comparison matrices, positioning documents, market research synthesis
- Metrics & Analytics: Success metrics frameworks, dashboard designs, KPI definitions, experiment designs
- User Stories & Acceptance Criteria: Detailed user stories with edge cases, test scenarios, workflow documentation
- Risk Assessment: Risk matrices, mitigation strategies, dependency mapping, launch readiness checklists
- Customer Feedback Processing: Categorizing feedback themes, sentiment analysis, identifying patterns across channels
- Structured Content: Templates, frameworks, checklists, workflows, comparison matrices
- Framework Creation: Decision matrices, prioritization models (RICE, MoSCoW), estimation templates
- Creative Writing: Stories, scripts, marketing copy, blog posts
- Technical Explanations: Breaking down complex concepts with examples and analogies
- Technical Discovery: Architecture considerations, API requirements, integration planning, scalability analysis
- Ideation & Brainstorming: Generating multiple approaches, exploring trade-offs, considering edge cases
- Meeting Preparation: Agenda creation, discussion guides, pre-read materials, retrospective formats

**What Requires Manual Verification:** (when applicable)
- Product Assumptions: Test core assumptions about user behavior and value proposition with real users
- Always validate statistics, market data, and research citations
- User Research Data: Confirm AI analysis against original transcripts and raw feedback
- Competitive Intelligence: Verify competitor features, pricing, and positioning with direct product testing
- Market Size/TAM: Cross-reference market data with multiple analyst reports and industry sources
- Customer Quotes: Ensure customer feedback summaries accurately represent original sentiment and context
- Success Metrics Baselines: Confirm current performance numbers with analytics team before setting targets
- Technical Feasibility: Validate engineering estimates, architecture suggestions, and complexity assessments with dev team
- Integration Requirements: Validate third-party API capabilities, rate limits, and pricing with vendors
- Compliance Requirements: Review GDPR, CCPA, SOC2, HIPAA recommendations with legal and security teams
- Cost Estimates: Verify infrastructure, licensing, and operational costs with finance and engineering
- Launch Timelines: Confirm engineering effort estimates and dependency timelines with development team
- User Segments: Validate persona definitions and segment characteristics with user research and data teams
- Accessibility Claims - Test WCAG compliance and screen reader compatibility with accessibility specialists
- Performance Requirements - Benchmark SLA targets against actual system capabilities and monitoring data



**Always:**
- ✅ Test hypothesis, theories, recommendations when possible
- ✅ Cross-reference data with industry reports
- ✅ Update analysis based on new information
- ✅ Provide addtitional relevant supplemental docs/links
